Privacy Policy

Drift - Wind Seeds · Number Weave · One Line Maze · Weight Balance · Light Split  ·  Last updated: 21 October 2026

This policy covers the puzzle games listed on this site (“the apps”). They are casual games. They do not create user accounts and they do not ask for your name, email, or phone number.

Data stored on your device

Each app stores game progress locally on your device: unlocked levels or packs, stars, best times, tutorial or how-to flags, and the sound mute setting. This data never leaves your device. Clearing the app storage or uninstalling the app deletes it.

Advertising

The apps may show ads from Google AdMob (a banner, interstitial ads after some play, and optional rewarded ads for a hint, extra help, or to unlock content). Google and its partners may collect device identifiers, approximate location, and usage data to show and measure ads. See Google’s policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can reset or limit ad tracking in your device settings (Google Settings → Ads on Android, or Settings → Privacy & Tracking on iOS).

Children

The games are suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are served by Google; if you want a child-directed experience, use the device’s parental controls.

What we do not do

  • No sign-in, no cloud save, no analytics account of our own
  • No sale of personal data
  • No access to contacts, camera, microphone, or files
